---
name: Client sharing by organization
description: Product rule for whether collaborators share client records.
---

Collaborators should see all client records belonging to their organization. Client visibility is organization-scoped, not limited by creator or assignee.

**Why:** The user confirmed that organization-wide sharing is the intended behavior.

**How to apply:** Do not narrow client visibility to an individual collaborator. If a collaborator sees a client from another user, first check whether both accounts belong to the same organization; visibility across different organizations is not intended.
