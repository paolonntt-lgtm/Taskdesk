---
name: Shared workspace collaboration
description: Product rules for enabling external collaboration in additional workspaces.
---

Additional workspaces can become externally shared by inviting active accounts from other billing organizations. Workspace creation, invitations, share links, and member management belong in a separate administration area; the bottom-left workspace switcher is only for switching between accessible workspaces. External invitees must accept before gaining membership, and sharing cannot be disabled while external members remain; disabling it revokes outstanding invite links.

**Why:** The user requested external collaboration for additional organizations while keeping workspace switching distinct from workspace administration and preserving billing ownership.

**How to apply:** Keep the workspace/tenant identity separate from the external-sharing setting. New workspaces can start private, and explicit invitations or links enable collaboration without moving accounts or billing seats.
