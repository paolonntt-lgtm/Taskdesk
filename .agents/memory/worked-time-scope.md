---
name: Per-ticket worked-time opt-in
description: Product decision about how the ticket-level worked-time flag relates to the organization-wide setting.
---

A ticket-level flag is sufficient to enable worked-time tracking; it does not depend on the organization-wide setting.

**Why:** The user chose “Solo per-ticket” when asked whether the per-ticket flag should depend on the company setting.

**How to apply:** In future worked-time behavior, treat the ticket flag as an independent opt-in rather than gating it behind an organization-level toggle.
