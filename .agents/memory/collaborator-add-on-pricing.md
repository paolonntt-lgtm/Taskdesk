---
name: Collaborator add-on pricing
description: Confirmed per-seat price and interval behavior for TaskDesk collaborator add-ons.
---

Charge each additional collaborator at €6/month or €60/year. The add-on interval must always match the base subscription interval.

**Why:** The user chose these prices and specified that collaborator billing should follow the subscription cadence.

**How to apply:** Select the add-on Stripe price from the base subscription's recurring interval. Do not bill monthly for annual plans or leave an add-on on a previous cadence after a plan change.
