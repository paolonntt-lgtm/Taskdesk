description: Product expectations for capturing and reviewing notes during report preparation.

During report preparation, preserve collaborator notes across turns even when written as ordinary sentences, and show the complete draft for review before publishing. Use Sì/No quick replies while preparing; once the preview is ready, offer Modifica report and Invia report. Show report shortcuts at chat start or after an explicit report request, not after ordinary turns; avoid an open-ticket prompt on report summaries.

**Why:** The user reported that Albert was not reliably recognizing collaborator notes, asked for faster replies during preparation and clear edit/send choices after reviewing the draft, and noted that repeated report and ticket shortcuts distract from ordinary summaries.

**How to apply:** Do not rely only on the short chat history for report notes; make the prepared content visible so collaborators can verify and refine it before publication. Keep action shortcuts contextual to the user's current intent.
