---
name: TaskDesk help guides
description: Product requirement for organizing and maintaining in-app help content.
---

Keep the general TaskDesk guide concise, scannable, and available in Italian and English. Keep the detailed TaskTrack explanation as its own focused guide, and link to it from the general guide where appropriate.

**Why:** The user asked first for less-discursive TaskTrack help, then for help covering the rest of the product without losing that focused guide.

**How to apply:** When documenting new user-facing features, add short localized guidance to the relevant general-help section rather than folding unrelated material into the TaskTrack guide. From a supported feature page, link directly to that section.
