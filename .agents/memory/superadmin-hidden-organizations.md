---
name: Hidden organizations in superadmin
description: Product rule for hiding organizations from the superadmin panel.
---

Hiding an organization from the superadmin list is a reversible visibility choice only. It must not delete organization data or change access for its users; superadmins must be able to find and restore hidden organizations.

**Why:** The user prefers hiding test organizations over deleting them so their records remain intact.

**How to apply:** Keep the hidden state scoped to the superadmin list and provide a path to view and unhide organizations.
