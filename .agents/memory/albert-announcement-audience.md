---
name: Albert announcement audience
description: Audience and placement for Albert feature-release notices.
---

Albert feature announcements are intended for existing signed-in TaskDesk customers. Their primary surface should be a concise notice inside the authenticated app, with a direct way to open Albert and ask questions.

**Why:** The user clarified that the Albert update should reach customers when they log in, rather than being presented only as a public-facing marketing page.

**How to apply:** Keep future Albert feature-launch messaging inside the logged-in product unless the user specifically requests public acquisition content.
